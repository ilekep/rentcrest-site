Common questions

Where is my money kept? In your own bank or savings app. Rentcrest never holds, moves or touches your money. You log what you save.

Is my data backed up? Your plan is stored on your device and included in your normal iCloud or computer backup.

How do I cancel Rentcrest Plus? Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Rentcrest Plus.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the Rentcrest Plus screen in the app and tap Restore.

How do I delete my data? In the app, go to Settings → Delete all my data, or delete the app.
