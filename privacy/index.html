Rentcrest Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

The short version

Rentcrest keeps your rent plan, savings and documents on your iPhone or iPad. We never see them. We use two service providers: RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, and AppsFlyer to measure which ads bring people to the app. Rentcrest never holds, moves or touches your money.

What the app stores

Your goals, rent amounts, save dates, the savings you log, notes, reminder settings, your country/region choice, and any documents, receipts, photos, contacts and rental history you add. All of it is stored on your device, in the app's own storage. A small summary (progress and targets) is shared with the Rentcrest widget through an on-device App Group so the widget can show it.

What we collect

Your rent plan, savings, documents, photos and notes never leave your device. To run subscriptions and measure our ads we collect a limited set of data through RevenueCat and AppsFlyer (see below): an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history, device and app information (model, iOS version, app version, country, IP address), install and app-open events, and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier. We do not sell your data.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from "Help us get better", Rentcrest opens your own email app with your message, your app version, iOS version and country. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We receive that email and your email address and use them only to read and reply to you. The app also counts, on your device only, how many days you have used it, so it can ask regular users for feedback.

Update check

About once a day the app asks Apple's public App Store lookup whether a newer version of Rentcrest is available. Only the app's identifier and your store country are sent, to Apple. Nothing about you or your savings is included.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. We use RevenueCat to keep a record of them (which plan, when it renews or ends, refunds) linked to an anonymous ID, so we can see our revenue and help with purchase problems. RevenueCat never receives your payment card details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Ad measurement and AppsFlyer

We advertise on platforms such as TikTok, Meta (Facebook, Instagram), Pinterest, Snap and Google. AppsFlyer tells us which ad or campaign led to an install and a subscription, and shares that result with the ad platform so it can improve our ads. It receives device and app information, install and app-open events, sign-up and paywall views, and subscription revenue from RevenueCat. See appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Your tracking choice

After you subscribe, iOS asks whether Rentcrest may track you. If you allow it, your advertising identifier is shared with AppsFlyer and the ad platforms to match ads to installs. If you don't, they don't receive it, and we only get Apple's anonymous campaign reports. The app works the same either way, and you can change your choice in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Payments

Rentcrest Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether your subscription is active. We never receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Rentcrest only learns whether the check succeeded. Your biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave and is never available to us.

Backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups, your device backup may include Rentcrest's data. That is handled by Apple under your backup settings.

Your choices

You can export everything as CSV and JSON, or delete all data, from Settings in the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data from the device.

Children

Rentcrest is meant for adults (18+) managing their own rent.

Legal and money information

Rent rules and fee caps in the app are general information, not legal or financial advice. Always check the official source linked in the app.
